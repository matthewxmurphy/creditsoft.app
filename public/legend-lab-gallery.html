Signal meanings for the gallery

Use this only as a key. The labels below are written clearly enough that the legend can stay out of the way.

Negative Reporting Chargeoff, collection, bankruptcy, or another derogatory signal is present.
Missing Bureau The tradeline should exist, but at least one bureau is not carrying it.
Single Bureau Only one bureau is reporting this version of the record.
Mismatch Values differ across bureaus and need Metro 2 review.
High Utilization Utilization is over the coaching or review threshold.
Manual Review An operator should check the record before acting on it.

Quick decision guide

If you do not want to compare all twelve, use this summary and dismiss it after picking.

Calmest default Try 2 or 7 if you want readable labels without reverting to pills.
Best for dense tables Try 9 or 10 if the compare grid is going to carry many rows.
Most label-like Try 1, 4, or 10 if the markers should feel attached to the record.
Safest live-app choice Option 10 is the cleanest bridge from the current legend experiments into production UI.
