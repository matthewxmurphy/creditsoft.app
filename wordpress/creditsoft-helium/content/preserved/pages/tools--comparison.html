    How this tool works

    The monthly budget is all entered minimum payments plus extra. After minimums, remaining money goes to the highest APR (avalanche) or smallest balance (snowball). Payments freed by a payoff roll to the next debt, including within the same month. Assumes fixed APRs/minimums, monthly interest, no fees or new borrowing; maximum 600 months.

    Reference: CFPB debt repayment strategies.
