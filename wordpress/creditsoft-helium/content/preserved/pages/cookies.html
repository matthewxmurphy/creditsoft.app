Cookie Policy

How CreditSoft uses essential cookies, analytics cookies, marketing pixels, and consent controls.

Last Updated: February 2026

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to support site functionality, preferences, analytics, and marketing measurement.

Types We Use

  • Essential: Required for site functionality, security, account access, and checkout behavior.
  • Analytics: Help us understand site usage and improve pages.
  • Marketing: Used for relevant ads and conversion measurement when you consent.

Managing Cookies

You can accept or decline optional cookies through the banner. You can also control or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect functionality.

Compliance

CreditSoft's public site consent flow is intended to support GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, DMA, LGPD, POPIA, and Google Consent Mode v2.
